## Unreleased

### Added

- **Stream.** A small brook trickling through a wood, recorded by FractalStudios (CC0, via Wikimedia Commons).

### Changed

- **Real waves.** Waves are now a recording of a fjord shore in Denmark by Luftrum (CC BY 3.0, via Wikimedia Commons) instead of synthesis. Brown noise is the only sound still made in the browser.
- **Real wind.** Wind is now a recording of a storm in a Swedish pine forest by W.carter (CC BY-SA 4.0, Wikimedia Commons) instead of synthesis. The *About* sheet links licenses that require it.
- **Softer brown noise.** It is now deeper and calmer: a two-pole low-pass removes the hiss above a few hundred hertz, a gentle high-pass trims sub-bass that small speakers cannot play, and the tone drifts slowly so it breathes. The loop is 36 s instead of 24 s.

### Removed

- **Pink noise.** The deeper brown noise covers the same need. Saved mixes and share links that include pink noise still open, without it, and *Deep focus* is now brown noise with a little rain.

## 1.0.0 — 2026-11-17

The first public release. CozyMix was rewritten from scratch: code, interface, art direction, audio engine, tests and documentation.

### The idea

One sound is one pad, with its volume always visible. There are no panels to open, and no illustration that could contradict what you hear. A pad takes on its sound's colour when it is on, and its little bars move only while you can actually hear it.

### Features

- **Sound pads.** A tap turns a sound on or off, and the slider sets its volume. Moving the slider of a sound that is off turns it on; moving it to zero turns it off. Each pad shows its percentage, a loading state, and any error with a *Try again* button.
- **An always-visible player** at the bottom of the screen. It has play/pause, which glows with the real audio level, plus the mix name, the overall volume, the sleep timer, Save and Share.
- **Eight ready-made scenes**, including *Nebulizer night*, with *Undo* after every change.
- **Your mixes:** save one under a name, load it, or delete it with *Undo*. Empty and duplicate names are refused.
- **Readable share links** such as `#mix=rain.65,wind.15`. They never start playing on their own.
- **A sleep timer** that follows the wall clock and fades out over the last 30 seconds.
- **A "lantern" look**: plum night, amber light and serif headings, on a background tinted by the sounds that are on. A warm paper theme follows the system's light mode.
- **Hand-drawn icons** for every sound, and a crescent-moon app icon generated by a script.
- **English and Italian**, with no flash of the wrong language: the app renders in the browser, already in the right language.
- **Offline use** through a network-first service worker. While online you always get the latest version.

### Audio engine

- **Declarative.** The interface says what it wants to hear; the engine loads, starts, fades and stops sounds to match.
- **Seamless loops.** An equal-power crossfade is baked into each buffer.
- **Synthesized sounds.** Waves, wind, and brown and pink noise are made in the browser, in stereo. Their envelopes repeat exactly once per loop, so there is no seam.
- **Thunder** rolls in shortly after you turn it on, then at random intervals.
- **Volume and Safari.** The volume curve is more natural, and on Safari sound keeps playing when the ringer switch is on silent (`navigator.audioSession`).

### Publishing

- **Static export to GitHub Pages.** The site is deployed on every push to `main`, but only after lint, typecheck, the Node tests and the browser tests pass.
- **Works under a sub-path** such as `/cozymix/`. The browser tests run against exactly that build.
- **Polite caching.** The service worker deletes only its own caches, never those of other sites on `github.io`.
- **Local preview.** `npm run preview` serves the exported site the way Pages does.
- **No stale builds in development.** `npm run dev` removes any service worker and cache left by an earlier production build.

### Tests

- **30 Node tests** for the models and the DSP. They check the loops for seams, and check that the service worker and the catalog list the same audio files.
- **17 Playwright tests with real audio**, which measure the engine's output level. They cover:
  - accessibility with axe, in both themes;
  - layouts from 320 to 1440 px;
  - offline use;
  - the sleep timer, with a simulated clock.
